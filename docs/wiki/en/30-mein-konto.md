# 30 · My account

*For users.* At the bottom of the sidebar under **My account**: password, second factor and
language.

## Where your account comes from

What you can change here depends on where your account lives. The page tells you at the top.

| Your account comes from | Changing the password | Second factor |
|---|---|---|
| the **company directory** (AD/LDAP) | there, as is usual in your company. The new one then applies here too | in the central sign-in |
| the **central sign-in** | there. OTA sends you there and brings you back afterwards | in the central sign-in |
| **OTA itself** | here on the page | here on the page |

## Changing the password ✅

Only for accounts from OTA itself. You enter the current password and the new one twice, at least
12 characters.

A password change **signs out all your other sessions**, for example in the browser on another
computer. The session you are in stays. Your workspaces keep running.

## Second factor ✅

With a second factor, your password alone is no longer enough to get in.

**In the central sign-in** you set it up there, via the button on the page. You can choose:
- a **one-time code** from an authenticator app on your phone
- a **passkey**: fingerprint, face or a security key

**For accounts from OTA itself:**
1. Click **Set up two-factor**.
2. Scan the code with the authenticator app. If that does not work, enter the secret shown by hand.
3. Type in the six digits from the app.
4. Keep the **recovery codes** safe: print them or put them in your password manager. You see them
   only this once. With them you get in when your phone is gone; each works once.

If you run out of recovery codes, you renew them here. The old ones then stop working. Switching it
off requires a valid code, so nobody can remove it with your password alone.

**Required for your group?** Then there is a notice at the top, and until the second factor is set
up no workspace can be started. The **Set it up now** button leads here.

Lost your phone and no recovery codes left? The administrators reset the second factor. Then you
set it up again.

## Language ✅

German or English. The choice applies immediately and is remembered **on the account**; you do not
have to make it again on another computer. You can also switch it at the top right of every page.

## Theme

Light, dark or like your system: the switch at the bottom of the sidebar. It applies to this
browser.

## What you do not need to set

The **resolution** of your applications: the remote screen follows the size of your browser window
([chapter 34](34-bedienung.md)).
